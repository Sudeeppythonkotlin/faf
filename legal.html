Safety Guidelines

  • FAF is only for students aged 18 and above.
  • Be respectful. Harassment, hate, threats, spam and fake identities are not allowed.
  • Never send money, OTPs, passwords or bank details to anyone you meet here.
  • Before meeting anyone in person, meet in a public place and tell a friend or family member.
  • FAF has no chat. Once you connect on Instagram, Snapchat, LinkedIn or Discord, that platform's rules apply.
  • If someone behaves badly, stop talking to them, press FIND SOMEONE ELSE, and report them on the platform where you connected (Instagram, Snapchat, LinkedIn or Discord). You can also email us.
  • You can hide yourself any time in Settings (Show me to other students).

Privacy Policy

What we collect. Your Google account identifier and name (for sign-in), and what you add: display name, date of birth (we show only your age), gender, college name, university, state (taken from your university), interests, avatar, and the social usernames you choose to add. We also store your discovery history (who you were shown, for 30 days, so you don't see the same person again), and a daily usage counter. We also keep anonymous, combined usage counts (for example, how many searches happen each day).

What others can see. Only your display name, age, college, university, state, interests, avatar, and the social accounts you switched on. We never show your email, date of birth, phone number or exact location.

How we use it. To run FAF: to show you to other eligible students, to keep the service safe, and to improve it. We do not sell your personal data. Future advertising may be shown; if so we will say so here.

Control and deletion. You can edit your profile, hide yourself, or permanently delete your account in Settings. Deleting removes your profile and associated data from FAF.

Service providers. FAF runs on Google Firebase and Netlify, which process data on our behalf.

Age. FAF is for adults only. If we learn someone under 18 has an account, we will remove it.

Terms of Service

Your conduct. You will not harass, threaten, impersonate, scam or spam anyone, share unlawful content, scrape or copy data from FAF, or use bots or automated tools.

Your content. You own what you add, and you allow FAF to display it to other users as described in the Privacy Policy.

Connecting with others. FAF only introduces students. We do not verify identities, and we are not responsible for what happens between users on other platforms or in person. Use the safety tips above.

Suspension. We may suspend or remove accounts that break these terms or the safety guidelines.
